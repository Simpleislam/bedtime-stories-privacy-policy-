# Privacy Policy

**Muslim Kids Bedtime Duas & Stories** (Android)

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This app is made for families to use at bedtime. It is built to need nothing from you:
there is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

## The short version

**We do not collect, store or share any personal information about you or your children.**
There is no analytics, no advertising network, no crash reporting service and no third-party
software development kit in the app. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is
nowhere for it to be sent.

## What the app keeps on your phone

So that you can carry on where you left off, the app remembers a few things on the device itself:

- which dua card you were last on;
- which story series and story you last opened, and how many seconds into a narration you had reached;
- your chosen playback speed.

This is stored by the app on your own phone and is never uploaded anywhere. We cannot see it.
You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage
(**Settings → Apps → Muslim Kids Bedtime Duas & Stories → Storage → Clear data**) or by
uninstalling the app.

## When the app uses the internet

The bedtime duas — the recording, the Arabic, the transliterations and the meanings — are all
inside the app. That part works with no internet connection at all, including in aeroplane mode.

The app connects to the internet in only two situations:

1. **When you play a story narration.** The narrations are not stored inside the app; they are
   streamed from the Internet Archive (`archive.org`), where the publisher hosts them. When a
   narration plays, your device asks `archive.org` for that audio file, so the Internet Archive
   receives your device's IP address, which file was requested, and the usual technical details
   any website receives. We receive nothing. The Internet Archive's own privacy policy applies to
   that request: https://archive.org/about/terms.php
2. **If you tap the source link at the end of a story.** Each story card ends with a link to the
   page it came from on `uswatulmuslimah.co.za`. Nothing is loaded from that website unless you
   tap the link, and tapping it opens your phone's normal web browser, where that website's own
   privacy policy applies.

The written text of every story is inside the app, so you can read the stories without an
internet connection even when the narration cannot play.

## Permissions

The app asks for a single Android permission, **Internet**, used only for the two purposes above.
It does not ask for, and cannot use, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos,
files or phone identifiers.

## Children

The app is intended to be used by children alongside a parent. It collects no personal
information from anyone, including children. There are no adverts, no in-app purchases, no
accounts, no chat, no comments, no links to social networks, and no way for a child to send
anything to us or to anyone else from within the app.

## Content

The bedtime duas are recited from the Qur'an and Sunnah, with the source of each one shown in
the app. The bedtime stories and their narrations are the work of
[Uswatul Muslimah](https://uswatulmuslimah.co.za), and each story names the page it came from.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the date at the
top will change. Because the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification
rather than a change in what happens to your information.
